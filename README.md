# CAMPUS-EQUIPMENT-BORROWING-DATABASE-MANAGEMENT-SYSTEM
The proposed Campus Equipment Borrowing System provides a structured relational database for student borrowing requests, reservations, approvals, issue/return transactions, condition history, maintenance, fines and payments. 

PROBLEM STATEMENT : 
Design and implement a database for a Campus equipment borrowing service in which students can request and reserve equipment, authorized personnel can approve borrowing requests, equipment can be issued and returned through individual borrowing-item records, and the institution can maintain condition history, maintenance records, fines and payments. The database must enforce primary key, foreign key and domain constraints so that borrowing information remains consistent and traceable. 

OBJECTIVES:
1) Maintain student and department information for identifying borrowers.
2) Maintain equipment and category information with unique asset identification.
3) Record borrowing requests and individual equipment items under each request.
4) Support reservation and approval tracking.
5) Track equipment condition over time and associate observations with borrowing when
   applicable.
6) Record maintenance activities performed on equipment.
7) Assess fines for borrowing items and record corresponding payments.
8) Use normalization and relational constraints to reduce redundancy and preserve integrity.
